# Patent and Prior-Art Position

The project intends NauqCrypt specifications and implementations to be freely
usable. To the extent the project owner controls patent claims necessarily
infringed by implementing a released NauqCrypt specification, the owner will
not assert those claims against a conforming implementation, except defensively
in response to a patent claim asserted against the project or its users.

This statement is not a patent search, warranty, legal opinion, or guarantee of
freedom to operate. Published searches cannot cover unpublished applications,
unindexed material, or every jurisdiction. Every release must include its
search corpus, query log, cutoff date, known near neighbors, and limitations.
