# Prior-Art and Architecture-Originality Protocol

## Search A: publication and patent terminology

Search specifications, full papers, references, diagrams, claims, citations,
withdrawn revisions, and failed candidates across:

- CAESAR and NIST Lightweight Cryptography submissions.
- IACR ePrint, ToSC, FSE, CHES/TCHES, CRYPTO, EUROCRYPT, and ASIACRYPT.
- CFRG RFCs and active Internet-Drafts.
- Dissertations and older terminology around authenticated encryption,
  deterministic encryption, wide-domain ciphers, codes, and reversible nets.
- USPTO Patent Public Search, EPO Espacenet, and WIPO PATENTSCOPE.

Record databases, exact queries, dates, result identifiers, full-document
inspection decisions, backward/forward citations, CPC classes, and hashes.

## Search B: canonical dependency graph

Ignore the candidate vocabulary. Search by essential causal invariants, pass
structure, state lifetime, feedback, output timing, verification relation, and
proof shape. Maintain one near-neighbor row per essential invariant.

## Pass statement

A provisional pass requires two searches to locate no structural equivalent,
at least one essential invariant that survives canonicalization and supports a
new formal claim or measured advantage, and publication of the complete corpus
and exclusion rationale.

The output is not a legal freedom-to-operate opinion and never claims absolute
novelty.
